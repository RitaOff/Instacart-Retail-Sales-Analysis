# Instacart Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Instacart retail sales data using *PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI* to uncover sales trends, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and opportunities for business growth and operational efficiency.

The analysis uses SQL to answer key business questions and Power BI to present the results through an interactive dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies

- PostgreSQL
- pgAdmin 4
- SQL
- Power BI
- Data Analysis
- Data Visualization

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- *Total Orders:* 1.049M
- *Total Customers:* 63K
- *Total Revenue:* $158M
- *Average Order Value:* $151

## Business Questions

1. What are the top 5 departments by revenue?
2. What are the top 3 products sold most during weekends?
3. Does Bakery generate more profit than Alcohol?
4. Which products have not been sold?
5. What trends can be identified in sales over time?
6. What times of the day experience the highest order volume?

## Key Insights

- *Personal Care* is the highest-performing department, generating approximately *$20.8M in revenue*.
- *Snacks* follows with approximately *$20.0M in revenue*.
- Pantry, Beverages, and Frozen are also among the top revenue-generating departments.
- *Bakery generated $480K in profit, compared with *$342K for Alcohol**.
- The analysis found *0 unsold products*, indicating that all products recorded sales.
- Weekend purchasing patterns highlight high-demand products that should be kept adequately stocked.
- Order-volume patterns can help improve staffing and inventory planning.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize inventory and promotions for high-performing departments such as Personal Care, Snacks, Pantry, Beverages, and Frozen.
- Consider expanding Bakery products and promotions because of its stronger profitability compared with Alcohol.
- Maintain sufficient inventory of popular weekend products to reduce missed sales opportunities.
- Align staffing levels with high-volume ordering periods to improve customer service.
- Use historical sales trends to improve demand forecasting and inventory planning.
